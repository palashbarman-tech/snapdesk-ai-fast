Employee Remote Work and Device Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2025. This policy applies to all full-time employees of Nova Systems Pvt Ltd.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal. Contact hr@novasystems.in or call +91 9876543210 for questions.

Device allowance
Each employee receives a one-time device allowance of Rs. 85,000 for a laptop. Laptops must be replaced after 4 years. Damaged devices must be reported within 48 hours. The IT helpdesk is available at https://helpdesk.novasystems.in.

Data privacy
Confidential documents must be stored only on company-approved local drives or encrypted storage. Employees must not upload customer data to public AI services. On-device AI tools approved by IT may be used with internal documents.

Leave and deadlines
Annual leave is 24 days per year. Expense claims must be filed by 30 June 2025 for the first quarter, with a reimbursement limit of $500 per month for internet costs.
